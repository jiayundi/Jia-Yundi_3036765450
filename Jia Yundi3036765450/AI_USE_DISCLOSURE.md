## 2. How I Checked AI-Generated Code, Analysis and Writing

**Code checks.** I requested checks of data provenance, samples and repeat execution and used Codex to implement them as inspectable program outputs. The workflow compares the course CSV with the author's public data cell by cell and checks state counts, identifiers, missing observations and closure handling for the 410 restaurants. It cross-checks regression estimates using NumPy least squares and tests the equivalence of balanced-sample DID and first differences. It includes 21 analytical checks and six unit tests. Rerunning the final workflow in another directory reproduced all 18 final data, table and image files with identical hashes. These are automated verification results, rather than a claim that I manually checked every line of code.

**Analysis checks.** I specified the original tables and figure as replication targets and used comparison tables to examine sample sizes, variable definitions, coefficients and standard errors. Discrepancies were retained instead of adjusting observations to reproduce printed numbers. Through discussions with ChatGPT, I distinguished the relative employment change in this sample from a general policy conclusion or an ownership mechanism. The report explains that a single pre-policy wave cannot support a pre-trends test and that resampling restaurants within states does not directly provide state-level policy randomization inference.

**Writing checks.** I used ChatGPT to revise the text and Codex to cross-check the narrative against estimated outputs and source materials. This included checking that sample sizes, main coefficients, standard errors and statements about statistical significance agreed with the tables; that descriptions of the research question, policy timing and design were supported by the paper; and that the conclusions did not turn positive point estimates into universal policy effects or an imprecise ownership interaction into an established mechanism. The report retained the relevant qualifications. The revised PDF was rendered to check text, tables, figures and pagination. Writing checks therefore addressed the relationship between claims and evidence, as well as language.
